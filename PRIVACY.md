# Privacy Policy

Trust the Process does not collect, transmit, or sell any data.

- **What it stores:** your settings (headline, subtitle, which accounts to block, which shortcuts to show, snooze durations) and the time your current "Open email anyway" window ends.
- **Where:** in Chrome's extension storage. Settings use `chrome.storage.sync`, so Chrome may sync them across your signed-in browsers. Nothing is sent to the extension's authors or any third party.
- **What it reads:** on `mail.google.com` only, the tab title, to tell which account is open when you've limited blocking to specific addresses. It never reads your email content.
- **Network:** the extension makes no network requests of its own.
